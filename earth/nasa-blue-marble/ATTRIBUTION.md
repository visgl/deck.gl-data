# NASA Blue Marble

**Image credit: NASA Earth Observatory and NASA GIBS/ESDIS.**

`blue-marble-2048x1024.jpg` is an unmodified NASA GIBS WMS response for the
`BlueMarble_ShadedRelief_Bathymetry` layer. It is a global geographic image
covering longitude −180° to 180° and latitude −90° to 90°, at 2048 × 1024 pixels
in EPSG:4326. It includes shaded topography and ocean bathymetry.

## Sources

- [Blue Marble: Next Generation with Topography and Bathymetry](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/blue-marble-next-generation/base-topography-bathymetry/)
- [Exact NASA GIBS image request](https://gibs.earthdata.nasa.gov/wms/epsg4326/best/wms.cgi?SERVICE=WMS&REQUEST=GetMap&VERSION=1.1.1&LAYERS=BlueMarble_ShadedRelief_Bathymetry&STYLES=&FORMAT=image%2Fjpeg&SRS=EPSG%3A4326&BBOX=-180%2C-90%2C180%2C90&WIDTH=2048&HEIGHT=1024)
