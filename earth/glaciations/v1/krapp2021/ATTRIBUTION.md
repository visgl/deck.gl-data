vis.gl distributes the original NetCDF unchanged and converts its complete ice/ocean/land and maximum-land masks to Parquet. Added fields expose source indices, coordinates, positive ages and original time values; native values and precision are preserved. No new reconstructed extents, thickness or volume are supplied. Conversion code is MIT licensed.

Underlying reconstructions credited by the upstream NetCDF: ICE-6G (0–122 ka), Ganopolski & Calov (2011; 123–799 ka), and Spratt & Lisiecki (2016) global sea level. See the linked article and embedded source attributes for methodology and underlying references.
