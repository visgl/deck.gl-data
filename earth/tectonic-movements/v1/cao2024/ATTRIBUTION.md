# CAO2024 Parquet snapshot

Xianzhi Cao, Alan Collins, Sergei Pisarevsky, Nicolas Flament, Sanzhong Li, Derrick Hasterok and Dietmar Müller (2024).

Source: https://zenodo.org/records/13628813 (version 2.4).

License: [CC-BY-4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). These data files are not relicensed as MIT.

Converted by math.gl: present-day GPML polygons to GeoParquet, and finite rotations to 10 Ma quaternion samples. No source geometry simplification. See manifest.json for input/output checksums, exact frame, schemas, missing-data policy and conversion tools.

This snapshot represents this pinned model revision, not the mutable live GPlates service. Geometry and rotations share one tagged Parquet file.
